Our Introduction

Mokib 313 is a volunteer-based and donation-supported initiative dedicated to serving the Zawwar of Imam Hussain (A.S.) during the sacred journey of Arbaeen.

Serving the Zawwar of Imam Hussain (A.S.)

Mokib 313 was established in 2021 with a simple purpose: to serve the pilgrims travelling towards Karbala during Arbaeen.

Located at Pole 313 on the Najaf–Karbala route in Iraq, our volunteers work together to provide refreshments, water, tea, fruits, snacks, WiFi, mobile charging and practical support to the Zawwar.
