About Mokib 313

Service with sincerity, discipline, respect and compassion. Learn more about who we are and why we serve.

Serving the Zawwar of Imam Hussain (A.S.)

Mokib 313 was established in 2021 as a volunteer-based and donation-supported initiative serving pilgrims travelling towards Karbala during Arbaeen.

Our service point is located at Pole 313 on the Najaf–Karbala route in Iraq, where volunteers come together to support the Zawwar with essential refreshments, hospitality and practical assistance.

The purpose of Mokib 313 is simple: to serve with sincerity, discipline, respect and compassion.

Our Core Values

Sincerity

We serve with sincere intention and without expecting recognition or reward.

Service

We believe that serving the Zawwar is a responsibility and an honour.

Respect

Every visitor is treated with patience, dignity and respect.

Teamwork

Volunteers work together so every part of the Mokib runs smoothly.

Responsibility

We take responsibility for the services, resources and trust placed in our team.

Hospitality

We aim to make every visitor feel welcomed, supported and cared for.
