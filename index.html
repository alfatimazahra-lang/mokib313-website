About Us

Service with sincerity, unity & devotion

Mokib 313 is a volunteer-based initiative established to serve the Zawwar of Imam Hussain (A.S.) during the Arbaeen journey.

Located at Pole 313 on the Najaf–Karbala route, our volunteers provide essential services — from drinking water and Indian masala tea to refreshments, fruits, snacks, WiFi and mobile charging.
